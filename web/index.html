    About UK Power Cuts

    One live map of power cuts across every electricity network in England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland: UK Power Networks, National Grid Electricity Distribution, SSEN, SP Energy Networks, Northern Powergrid, Electricity North West and NIE Networks. Check your postcode to see power cuts near you, filter by network, and replay the last 7 days.

    Is there a power cut near me?

    Type your postcode into the search box. The map zooms to your area and tells you about any power cuts reported within 3 km, including whether your postcode is listed.

    Who do I call in a power cut?

    In England, Scotland and Wales call 105, free from any phone. In Northern Ireland call NIE Networks on 03457 643 643. If there is danger to life, such as fallen power lines, stay well away and call 999.

    Which electricity network covers my area?

    UK Power Networks: London, the South East and East of England. National Grid Electricity Distribution: the Midlands, South West England and South Wales. SSEN: the north of Scotland and central southern England. SP Energy Networks: central and southern Scotland, Merseyside, Cheshire and North Wales. Northern Powergrid: North East England and Yorkshire. Electricity North West: North West England. NIE Networks: Northern Ireland. Hover over or tap a network chip on the map to see its area.

    Power cuts by network

    UK Power Networks, National Grid Electricity Distribution, SSEN, SP Energy Networks, Northern Powergrid, Electricity North West, NIE Networks. Or browse by city.

    How up to date is the map?

    It reads every network's public power cut feed every 5 minutes. Networks can take a while to list a new fault, and very small faults may not appear.

    Can I see power cuts from earlier in the week?

    Yes. Drag the slider under the map to replay the last 7 days.

    Is this an official site?

    No. UK Power Cuts is an independent, open-source project that combines the networks' own published data. It is not affiliated with any electricity network or supplier.
